In Mumbai's heat and humidity, cooling and laundry machines run almost constantly. When an air conditioner stops cooling during peak summer, a refrigerator leaks, or a washing machine drum stops spinning, you need a technician who shows up on time, diagnoses the actual root cause honestly, and quotes transparent charges before beginning work — without pushing unnecessary spare parts or hidden fees.
